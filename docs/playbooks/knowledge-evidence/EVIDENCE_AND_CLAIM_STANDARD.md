# Evidence and Claim Standard

## Purpose

Important claims must be supported by evidence appropriate to their risk. Confidence, memory or AI output is not evidence.

## Claim classes

- **Operational claim** — a workflow or control works.
- **Technical claim** — a system behaves as stated.
- **Commercial claim** — a result, saving, capability or reference is true.
- **Compliance claim** — a requirement or control is satisfied.
- **Design claim** — an experience meets an agreed quality or usability bar.

## Evidence strength

From strongest to weakest:

1. direct production or controlled test evidence,
2. reproducible system record,
3. approved client or stakeholder record,
4. reviewed analysis based on primary data,
5. expert judgement with assumptions recorded,
6. anecdote or unverified statement.

The required strength increases with financial, safety, legal, reputational and operational risk.

## Evidence requirements

Evidence must identify:

- the exact claim it supports,
- source and location,
- date and environment,
- relevant commit, version or data period,
- collector or reviewer,
- limitations and unresolved uncertainty.

Old evidence does not automatically prove a new version, environment or workflow.

## Prohibited practices

Do not:

- reuse screenshots without version context,
- present estimates as measured outcomes,
- turn client praise into quantified performance claims,
- cite AI-generated text as a source,
- omit contradictory evidence,
- claim completion based only on implementation activity.

## Conflicting evidence

When evidence conflicts, the conflict must be visible. The accountable owner records the competing sources, assesses reliability and decides whether the claim is accepted, narrowed or withdrawn.

## Public claims

Case studies, proposals and marketing claims require traceable approval and must respect client confidentiality. Sensitive evidence may remain private while the public claim uses an approved, supportable formulation.

## Expiry

Evidence expires when the system, environment, workflow, data period or acceptance basis materially changes.
