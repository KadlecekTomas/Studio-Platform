# Performance and Growth Standard

## Purpose

Create a fair, evidence-based system for expectations, feedback, capability growth and intervention.

## Performance model

Performance is assessed against role outcomes and operating behaviours, not visibility, hours online or personal proximity to leadership.

Evidence should cover:

- outcomes delivered;
- quality and reliability;
- ownership and decision-making;
- client and team impact;
- adherence to standards;
- learning and capability growth;
- contribution to reusable knowledge.

## Expectation setting

Every person must know:

- expected outcomes for the period;
- priority order;
- decision authority;
- quality and evidence requirements;
- dependencies and constraints;
- review date.

Expectations that materially change must be updated explicitly.

## Feedback cadence

Feedback should be timely and specific. Material concerns must not be saved for a formal review.

Regular check-ins cover:

- progress and outcomes;
- blockers and capacity;
- quality signals;
- feedback in both directions;
- capability goals;
- support required.

## Capability levels

Capability is demonstrated through increasing scope, autonomy, judgement and reliability. Tenure alone does not establish level.

A level decision should consider whether the person can:

- handle expected complexity;
- identify and communicate risk;
- make sound decisions within authority;
- produce durable evidence;
- improve systems, not only complete tasks;
- help others become effective.

## Growth plan

A growth plan must identify:

- target capability;
- business reason;
- practical opportunities to demonstrate it;
- support or mentoring;
- evidence of progress;
- review date.

Training without application is not sufficient evidence of capability.

## Performance concern

A material performance concern must be documented with:

- unmet expectation;
- concrete examples and impact;
- prior feedback;
- required change;
- available support;
- review period;
- consequence if the gap remains.

The process must distinguish between capability, clarity, capacity, conduct and systemic constraints.

## Recognition

Recognition should connect contribution to impact. Rewarding repeated rescue work without addressing the underlying system is discouraged.

## Calibration

Leadership periodically reviews performance decisions for consistency, bias, role clarity and evidence quality.

## Prohibited practices

Do not:

- rate personality instead of outcomes and behaviours;
- use undocumented expectations retrospectively;
- treat sustainable boundary-setting as low commitment;
- reward avoidable heroics over reliable systems;
- allow AI-generated evaluation text without accountable human judgement.
