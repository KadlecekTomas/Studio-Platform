# Role and Accountability Standard

## Purpose

Define clear ownership so Studio Platform does not depend on assumptions, title inflation or invisible work.

## Core rule

Every recurring responsibility, project outcome and operating control must have one accountable owner. Contributors may be many; accountability is singular.

## Required role definition

Each role must state:

- purpose and business outcome;
- owned decisions;
- recurring responsibilities;
- required evidence and operating cadence;
- boundaries and exclusions;
- escalation path;
- capability expectations;
- backup or continuity arrangement.

## Ownership model

Use these distinctions consistently:

- **Accountable owner** — answerable for the outcome and final decision.
- **Responsible contributor** — performs defined work.
- **Reviewer or approver** — independently checks a required gate.
- **Consulted party** — provides expertise before a decision.
- **Informed party** — receives the decision or status.

A person must not approve their own high-risk work where independent review is required.

## Decision rights

Role definitions must identify which decisions the role may make independently, which require consultation and which require escalation.

Decision authority must be proportional to:

- impact on clients;
- financial exposure;
- security and privacy risk;
- reversibility;
- contractual consequences;
- operational blast radius.

## Role overlap

When two roles appear to own the same outcome, the conflict must be resolved explicitly. Shared accountability without a final owner is not accepted.

## Temporary ownership

Temporary or acting ownership must include:

- start and expected end date;
- exact delegated authority;
- excluded decisions;
- handback condition;
- named backup.

## Founder dependency

Responsibilities held only by the founder must be recorded as a continuity risk when they affect delivery, revenue, production access, client trust or critical knowledge.

The mitigation must be one or more of:

- documented procedure;
- delegated backup;
- restricted but recoverable access;
- training and shadowing;
- external specialist arrangement.

## Review cadence

Role definitions are reviewed:

- when a person joins or leaves;
- when the operating model changes;
- after a material incident;
- when repeated ownership confusion appears;
- at least quarterly for critical roles.

## Exit criteria

A role is operationally defined only when its owner can explain the outcome, authority, evidence, escalation path and backup without relying on unwritten context.
