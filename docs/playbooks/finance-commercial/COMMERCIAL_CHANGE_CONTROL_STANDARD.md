# Commercial Change Control Standard

## Purpose

Prevent informal requests from becoming unpriced commitments and preserve a clear boundary between contracted delivery, defects and new scope.

## Classification

Every material request is classified as one of:

- contracted scope;
- defect or warranty correction;
- clarification with no delivery impact;
- new scope;
- client dependency or delay;
- internal improvement;
- urgent exception.

The classification and rationale must be recorded before implementation begins.

## Change assessment

A commercial change assessment includes:

- requested outcome;
- business reason;
- affected requirements and deliverables;
- implementation effort and external cost;
- schedule, capacity and dependency impact;
- operational, security and support impact;
- price and billing mechanism;
- assumptions, exclusions and acceptance criteria;
- consequence of declining or postponing the change.

## Approval gate

New scope may enter delivery only after the authorized client and Studio Platform owners approve the commercial treatment. Approval must be durable and linked to the delivery record.

Silence, meeting attendance, prototype feedback or developer effort is not commercial approval.

## Urgent exceptions

Emergency work may start before full commercial approval only when delay would cause material harm. The exception must identify:

- emergency owner;
- immediate scope limit;
- maximum authorized exposure;
- reason normal approval cannot wait;
- retrospective approval deadline.

## Scope swaps

A scope swap is valid only when removed and added work are explicitly identified and their cost, schedule and acceptance implications are agreed. Calling new work a priority change does not make it cost-neutral.

## Rejected and deferred changes

Rejected or deferred requests remain documented with decision, reason and reconsideration trigger. They must not remain as hidden expectations.

## Closure

A change is closed when approval, delivery evidence, acceptance, invoicing treatment and impact on the project forecast are all recorded.
