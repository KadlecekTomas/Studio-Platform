# Invoicing and Collection Standard

## Purpose

Ensure earned revenue becomes accurate invoices and collected cash without avoidable delay.

## Invoice readiness

An invoice may be issued only when its trigger is evidenced by one or more of:

- signed contract milestone;
- accepted deliverable;
- approved timesheet or usage record;
- recurring service period;
- approved change request;
- documented advance-payment obligation.

The invoice basis must be traceable to the project source of truth.

## Required invoice data

Before issuance, verify:

- legal entity and billing address;
- tax identifiers and tax treatment;
- purchase order or client reference;
- currency and exchange-rate rule;
- contractual payment terms;
- milestone description and period;
- amount, tax and total;
- recipient and delivery channel;
- supporting evidence required by the client.

## Separation of duties

The person preparing an invoice must not unilaterally change contracted price, payment terms or bank details. Material corrections require independent approval.

## Invoice lifecycle

Every invoice is tracked through:

1. planned;
2. ready for evidence;
3. approved;
4. issued;
5. delivered;
6. due;
7. paid, disputed or overdue;
8. reconciled.

## Collections

Before due date, confirm successful delivery and absence of a known dispute. Once overdue:

- record the overdue amount and age;
- contact the agreed client owner;
- distinguish administrative delay from commercial dispute;
- assign next action and date;
- escalate according to materiality and age;
- pause additional uncontracted exposure where appropriate.

## Disputes and credit notes

A disputed invoice requires a documented reason, owner, evidence and resolution path. Credit notes, write-offs and payment-term extensions require explicit commercial approval and must preserve the audit trail.

## Reconciliation

Cash receipt must be matched to the correct invoice and project. Partial payments, fees, currency differences and unidentified payments must remain visible until resolved.
