# IP, Licence and Third-Party Standard

## Purpose

This standard protects client rights, Studio Platform reusable assets and the lawful use of software, content, models, fonts, media, datasets and external services.

## Ownership classes

Every material asset must be classified as one of:

- client-provided material,
- client-specific deliverable,
- Studio Platform pre-existing IP,
- Studio Platform reusable improvement,
- third-party proprietary asset,
- open-source component,
- AI-generated or AI-assisted material.

## Core rule

Ownership, licence scope and transfer conditions must be explicit before an asset is delivered, published or reused.

## Pre-existing and reusable IP

Studio Platform must not unintentionally transfer ownership of its frameworks, templates, generic components, methods, internal tooling or know-how when granting rights to a client-specific deliverable.

Client-specific confidential information must never be embedded into reusable assets.

## Open-source software

Before adoption, assess:

- licence type and obligations,
- compatibility with the delivery and distribution model,
- notice or attribution requirements,
- source-disclosure or copyleft implications,
- maintenance and security health,
- replacement feasibility,
- restrictions on commercial or hosted use.

Unknown licence status blocks release.

## Media, fonts and datasets

Do not assume that online availability grants commercial rights. Record the source, licence, permitted use, attribution duty, territory, duration and proof of acquisition.

## AI-assisted assets

AI output must be reviewed for:

- confidential input exposure,
- identifiable copying or trademark risk,
- uncertain ownership or provider restrictions,
- factual provenance where claims are involved,
- licence compatibility with the target use,
- required human transformation and approval.

AI output is not automatically safe to commercialise.

## Third-party services

For critical vendors and subprocessors, record contractual owner, service purpose, data access, dependency risk, termination path, export capability and material terms affecting the client.

## Delivery and handover

The handover record must distinguish assets transferred to the client, assets licensed to the client, third-party dependencies the client must maintain and Studio Platform assets excluded from transfer.
