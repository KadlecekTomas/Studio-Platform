# Contract and Obligation Standard

## Purpose

This standard ensures that Studio Platform accepts work only when contractual obligations, decision rights, delivery assumptions and risk allocation are understood and recorded.

## Core rule

No material delivery commitment may rely only on an email summary, call memory or informal promise. The authoritative agreement must be identifiable and accessible to the accountable owner.

## Required contract baseline

Before delivery starts, record:

- contracting parties and authorised contacts,
- scope and explicit exclusions,
- acceptance mechanism and acceptance period,
- payment milestones and due dates,
- client dependencies and required inputs,
- intellectual-property ownership and licence terms,
- confidentiality obligations,
- personal-data roles and processing terms,
- warranty, support and service-level obligations,
- liability limits and material exclusions,
- termination and transition obligations,
- governing law and dispute path,
- approved subcontractors or third parties where relevant.

## Obligation register

Every material obligation must have:

- a plain-language description,
- source document and clause reference,
- accountable owner,
- due date or recurring cadence,
- evidence required for completion,
- consequence of non-performance,
- status: planned, active, fulfilled, waived, superseded or disputed.

## Contract hierarchy

Where documents conflict, the owner must identify the controlling order of precedence. Assumptions, proposals and meeting notes cannot silently override signed terms.

## Non-standard terms

Legal or leadership review is required before accepting terms involving:

- unlimited or disproportionate liability,
- uncapped indemnities,
- penalties or service credits,
- broad warranties of legal compliance,
- transfer of pre-existing Studio Platform IP,
- exclusivity or non-compete restrictions,
- mandatory audits or security certifications,
- unusual data-residency or deletion duties,
- client rights to source code or infrastructure credentials,
- obligations dependent on third parties outside Studio Platform control.

## Change control

A scope change is not contractually effective until its commercial, delivery, legal and data impacts are recorded and approved by authorised parties.

## Closure

A project may be legally closed only when contractual deliverables, notices, handover duties, licence transfers, data-return or deletion duties and outstanding disputes are resolved or explicitly accepted as open items.
