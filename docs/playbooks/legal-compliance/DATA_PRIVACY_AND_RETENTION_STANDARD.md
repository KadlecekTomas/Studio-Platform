# Data Privacy and Retention Standard

## Purpose

This standard defines the minimum controls for personal data and other regulated or sensitive information handled during discovery, delivery, support and project closure.

## Core rule

Studio Platform collects and processes only data that is necessary for a defined purpose, under an identified legal or contractual basis, for no longer than required.

## Data baseline

Before processing begins, identify:

- data categories and data subjects,
- business purpose,
- controller and processor roles,
- lawful or contractual basis,
- systems and storage locations,
- access roles,
- subprocessors and cross-border transfers,
- retention period and deletion trigger,
- security classification,
- applicable client instructions.

## Prohibited behaviour

Do not:

- use production personal data in local development without explicit approval and safeguards,
- copy datasets into unapproved AI tools, chats or personal storage,
- retain exports because they may be useful later,
- collect fields without a documented product or legal need,
- use real identities in screenshots, demos or public evidence,
- silently repurpose data for analytics, marketing or model training.

## Privacy by design

Design must consider minimisation, role-based access, secure defaults, auditability, export, correction, deletion, retention and incident response before implementation is accepted.

## Test and demo data

Synthetic or irreversibly anonymised data is the default. Pseudonymised data remains protected data and must be treated accordingly.

## Retention

Every material dataset must have:

- an owner,
- a retention rule,
- a deletion or anonymisation mechanism,
- exceptions required by law or contract,
- evidence that the rule is executable.

Indefinite retention is not an acceptable default.

## Data-subject and client requests

Requests for access, correction, export, restriction or deletion must be routed to the accountable privacy owner, logged and completed within the applicable contractual or legal window.

## Incident linkage

Suspected unauthorised access, disclosure, loss or alteration of protected data enters the Incident Response Standard immediately. Legal notification requirements must be assessed without waiting for complete technical certainty.

## Project closure

At closure, confirm return, migration, deletion or continued authorised retention of client data, backups, exports, credentials and support copies.
