# Capability Map

**Úroveň:** L3 — Operating Tool  
**Stav:** v1.0  
**Vlastník:** Founder

## Purpose

Capability Map popisuje, co Studio Platform skutečně umí opakovaně dodat, na jaké úrovni a s jakým důkazem. Není to seznam technologií ani marketingový wishlist.

## Rating scale

- **0 — None:** bez praktické zkušenosti.
- **1 — Emerging:** jednorázová nebo částečná zkušenost.
- **2 — Usable:** schopnost lze dodat s přímým dohledem zakladatele.
- **3 — Strong:** opakovatelný proces, reference a standard kvality.
- **4 — Scalable:** schopnost zvládne více lidí bez ztráty kvality.
- **5 — Distinctive:** prokazatelná konkurenční výhoda Studio Platform.

## Usage rules

Capability Map must be consulted when:

- accepting a project,
- designing a proposal,
- deciding what to market,
- planning hiring,
- selecting internal investments,
- claiming expertise publicly.

## Selling rule

Studio Platform may actively lead with capabilities rated 3 or higher.

Capabilities rated 2 may be sold when:

- risks are transparent,
- scope is controlled,
- senior ownership is explicit,
- the project creates strategic evidence.

Capabilities rated 0–1 must not be marketed as established expertise.

## Evidence requirements

Acceptable evidence includes:

- production project,
- measurable client outcome,
- approved case study,
- reusable playbook,
- automated verification,
- independent contributor delivery,
- operational history.

A repository, demo or AI-generated prototype alone is not sufficient proof of a strong capability.

## Review cadence

Update:

- after every Project Harvest,
- before annual planning,
- before launching a new service,
- after significant hiring.

## Anti-patterns

- rating based on confidence rather than evidence,
- treating a technology keyword as a business capability,
- selling weak capabilities as mature,
- keeping low-value capabilities only because they are interesting,
- raising levels after documentation without real delivery.

## Strategic objective

Build a narrow set of distinctive capabilities around reliable operational systems before expanding into unrelated services.
